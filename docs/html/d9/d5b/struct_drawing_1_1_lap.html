Detailed Description

This is a simple structure for storing the information about an overlap or sidelap on a particular drawing.

On a side tensioned mat, the left and right are considered "sidelaps" and the top and bottom "overlaps", and on an end tensioned mat, the left are right are considered "overlaps" and the top and bottom "sidelaps". When refering to top and bottom laps, top is Side::LEFT and bottom is Side::RIGHT.
